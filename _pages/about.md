⸻I am Sunjae Kwon, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Prof. Hong Yu. My research focuses on natural language processing, particularly clinical NLP, lexical semantics, and large language models.

My work investigates how language models can extract, ground, and reason over information in text and multimodal data. I am especially interested in applications to healthcare, including understanding clinical documentation, supporting patient education, and identifying risk factors from electronic health records.

Before joining UMass Amherst, I worked as an AI Research Scientist at KAIST and UNIST, and I was an AI Research Intern at Tencent America in 2023. I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science from Sogang University.

Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing: lexical semantics, information extraction, large language models, and multimodal grounding
- Clinical NLP: clinical information extraction, medical terminology, and patient education
- Medical Informatics: risk factor analysis, epidemiology, and clinical decision support

Selected Research

- ODD — A benchmark for detecting opioid-related aberrant behaviors in clinical text. NAACL 2024.
- Vision Meets Definitions — Unsupervised visual word sense disambiguation using gloss information. ACL 2023.
- MedJEx — Medical jargon extraction to support understanding of clinical language. EMNLP 2022.
- SAGE — Sense-aligned multimodal grounding evaluation with gloss-augmented reasoning. Preprint, 2026.
