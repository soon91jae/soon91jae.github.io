⸻About Me

I am Sunjae Kwon, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Prof. Hong Yu. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP), with an emphasis on clinical NLP, large language models, and lexical semantics.

I develop methods for extracting and reasoning over information in text and multimodal data, with applications in healthcare, patient education, and risk assessment. My interests span information extraction, word sense disambiguation, retrieval-augmented generation, and medical informatics.

Previously, I worked as an AI Research Intern at Tencent America (2023) and as an AI Research Scientist at KAIST (2019–2021) and UNIST (2017–2019). I received my M.S. (2017) and B.S. (2015) in Computer Science from Sogang University.

Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing: information extraction, lexical semantics, large language models, multimodal retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, and knowledge extraction.
- Clinical NLP: medical terminology extraction, clinical text understanding, patient education, and risk factor analysis.
- Medical Informatics: epidemiology, disease prediction, and clinical decision support.
